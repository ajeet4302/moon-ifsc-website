Our story

From a CA practice to a one-stop SEZ & IFSC consultancy

The MOON group was established in 2006 by CA Ratan Moondra, who began his Chartered Accountancy practice in 1997. The practice started with Auditing, Assurance, Taxation and Advisory, then moved into EPZ, STPI and EOU consultancy.

After the SEZ Act was introduced in 2005, the group founded Moon SEZ Consultants Private Limited. Today it offers one-stop solutions to all SEZ related requirements of developers, co-developers and units, and has worked on projects with MADC Mihan, MIDC, Hiranandani and Pipavav Shipyard, among others.
